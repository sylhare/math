# Kakeya: sources and links

## Scientific sources

- Hickman, *The Kakeya Conjecture: where does it come from and why is it important?*, arXiv:2512.09842
  (2025). https://arxiv.org/html/2512.09842v1
- Zahl, *A Survey of the Kakeya conjecture, 2000-2025*, arXiv:2512.09397. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.09397
- Wang, Zahl, *Volume estimates for unions of convex sets, and the Kakeya set conjecture in three
  dimensions*, arXiv:2502.17655 (2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17655
- Tao, *The three-dimensional Kakeya conjecture, after Wang and Zahl* (2025).
  https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2025/02/25/the-three-dimensional-kakeya-conjecture-after-wang-and-zahl/
- Guth, *The Kakeya conjecture, after Wang and Zahl*, arXiv:2604.03416. https://arxiv.org/html/2604.03416v1
- Guth, *Degree reduction and graininess for Kakeya-type sets in R^3*, arXiv:1402.0518.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.0518
- Falconer, *The Geometry of Fractal Sets* (CUP, 1985), pp. 96-99; Falconer, *Fractal Geometry*.
- Tao, *Recent progress on the restriction conjecture*, arXiv:math/0311181. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0311181
- Sogge, *Fourier Integrals in Classical Analysis*; Beltran, Hickman, Sogge (local smoothing).
- Fefferman, *The multiplier problem for the ball*, Ann. of Math. 94 (1971).
- Keich, *On L^p bounds for Kakeya maximal functions and the Minkowski dimension in R^2*.
- Wikipedia, *Kakeya set*: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakeya_set
